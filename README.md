# Focus – a gentle 30-day attention app

**Author: Dr. Greta Molo**

A low-effort, ADHD-friendly phone web app that goes with the workbook *Focus: A 30-Day Workbook to Train Your Attention*.

**Open it:** https://gretamolo.github.io/focus-app/. On a phone, use *Add to Home Screen* (Safari) or *Install app* (Chrome).

## What it does
- **Your daily set:** 3 short exercises a day by default (morning, before work, evening; about 5–10 min in total), shown as *Today 1/3, 2/3, 3/3*. When one is finished, a short success animation plays and the next card appears with **Start**. After the last one: *All done for today!* plus an optional **One more?**. You can choose 1–5 a day in settings, and 1 already counts as a good day. Your daily time budget (5/10/20 min) is shared across the set.
- **Real variety:** the exercises in each day's set always come from **different groups**, rotating across movement, brain games (Stroop, n-back, Schulte, go/no-go), get-started tools (2-minute start, brain dump, if-then, chunking), focus timers, environment/digital, attention and recharge breaks. **At most one breathing/calm exercise a day, never two days in a row**. No exercise repeats within 3 days, and “Something else” always switches to a different group.
- **Reminders:** in-app pop-up cards once a slot's time has passed and it isn't done (defaults 8:00, 12:00, 19:00; editable, each on/off) with *Start now* / *Later 1 h*. Local notifications are sent when you allow them (best effort, while the app is open or in the background). A single .ics file adds all the daily reminders to your calendar.
- **Today’s tasks (ADHD helper):** add a task in one tap. Pick a template (Laundry, Dishes, Tidy a room, Groceries, Pay bills, Email/call, Cook dinner, Kids’ things, Shower/self-care) or type your own, with an optional reminder time and optional .ics. **Now** shows one task at a time. Every task breaks into tiny 2–5 minute steps with a visual timer, the 2-minute-start rule, editable steps, practical ADHD tips (e.g. one load start-to-finish, drop-zone basket, clean as you go), and a small celebration for every step. Unfinished tasks roll over gently, with no “overdue”. There are optional **Morning reset (10 min)** and **Evening reset (15 min)** checklists.
- **Profiles:** a simple user switcher (name, emoji, colour) with fully separate data for each person: plan, exercises, tasks, reminders, check-ins and scores. Export one user or everyone.
- **Make it yours (per user):** exercises per day, time budget, reminder times on/off, which exercise groups to include, your own exercises and your own task templates.
- **Daily reminders (optional):** simple custom nudges such as water, vitamins or medication, with *Done* / *Later* pop-ups. They are included in the calendar file and off by default.
- **Health reminders (optional, per user, all off until chosen):** a new user is asked *Want reminders for…?* and toggles chips; everything can be changed later in More → Health reminders. Presets: **water** (goal ring, 8 glasses by default, nudges at 10/13/16 h or about every 3 h), **vitamins/supplements** (9:00, your own list, *All my usual*), **medication** (your time), **skin care AM/PM** (8:00 / 21:00, optional step list), **gym/movement** (18:00), **sleep wind-down** (22:00), **evening check-in** (20:00, mood and energy, plus *did you drink today?* only if the alcohol tracker is on), a **period tracker** (predicts the next start from logged starts, then asks *Did your period start?* each day until Yes, then *Did it end?* until Yes) and **weekly/interval routines** (hair mask, exfoliating, microneedling every 2 weeks, scalp needling weekly, weekly supplement, injections with site rotation; templates, care tips, Pause/Resume, Done/Later/Skip). Pop-up cards work the same way: *Done* logs it, *Later* waits 1 h. All of it is included in the calendar file with the right repeat rules, and there is a small weekly summary with a 7-day log and no shame cues. These are reminders only, not medical advice.
- **Big project planner:** add a big goal (e.g. *Sell my house*) with a target date or *in N months*. The app splits it into phases and tiny weekly steps across the timeline, and each step has mini sub-steps and a short tip. There are 10 offline templates: **Sell a house** (US-oriented: declutter, repairs, agent, comps and pricing, staging, photos, listing, showings, offers, inspection/appraisal, closing, moving), **Move home**, **Plan a renovation**, **Plan a trip**, **Start a side business**, **Organize finances/taxes**, **Deep declutter the home**, **Plan a party/event**, **Job search** and **Learn a skill**. There is also a **Custom project** with a helper (*What does done look like? What's the first tiny step? Split it into phases*). Steps rescale when the date changes, and a too-tight timeline gets a kind warning with a calmer date. **This week** shows 1–3 steps on Today, and tapping one opens its sub-step checklist with *Done / Next week / Skip / Edit*. You can reorder, add or delete steps, and **push the whole plan back a week** in one tap with no guilt. Each phase has a progress bar, and finishing a phase is celebrated. There is a weekly check-in reminder (default Mon 9:00) and optional per-step reminders, both through the pop-ups and the .ics file. You can have several projects per person. Template steps are in English and Lithuanian.
- **Rewards (ADHD-friendly, per user):** every completion gives instant feedback: a confetti burst, a varied cheer, a vibration where supported, and an optional short sound. Points are scaled to effort (harder exercises, tasks, task steps, project steps, phases and health check-ins), with **+5 for the first win of the day** and a bonus for starting a task marked *I'm dreading this one*. Occasional **surprise bonuses** (2× or a mystery box) appear. **Levels** unlock stickers and colour themes, and a growing plant with a jar fills up as you go. A **forgiving rhythm** allows up to 2 rest days per week and never uses "missed". There is an evening *Today's wins* summary and **My treats**, real-life rewards you price in points and redeem; spending never lowers your level. Animations, sound, vibration, surprises and the evening summary can each be switched off. Today only shows a small points counter and a level bar.
- **One-tap tracking:** one focus emoji per day. Everything else is optional and collapsed. Finishing an exercise, timer or drill logs itself.
- **No shame:** it counts *days practised this month* instead of streaks, missed days simply skip, and there's a friendly welcome back. Points and badges reward doing anything.
- **30-day plan:** Month 1 follows the workbook. *New month* builds a fresh varied plan. Days can be skipped or swapped.
- **79 exercises** in 8 groups with steps, time, difficulty and an honest “why it works” note with sources, plus in-app drills (Stroop, n-back 1/2/3, Schulte, go/no-go, reaction time, breathing pacer, Pomodoro with a distraction counter, guided 5-4-3-2-1).
- **Progress charts**, the Day 1 vs Day 30 quiz, English/Lithuanian interface, and JSON backup.

## Privacy
Everything is stored only in your browser (localStorage) on your device. There are no accounts, analytics or network requests. The page is a single self-contained HTML file with embedded fonts.

## About the evidence
The best-supported strategies for everyday focus are sleep, physical activity, distraction control, if-then plans, and CBT- or coaching-style skills. Mindfulness has modest effects. Brain-training games mainly improve the games themselves. This app supports, but does not replace, ADHD treatment: medication and CBT-based programmes have the strongest evidence. Please talk to a doctor. It is not medical advice.
